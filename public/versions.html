The brief. Land like pump.fun defaulted to a launchpad — big record artwork in the center. Commit to a chain (store takes over the UI) yet hop chains easily; swap & bridge never far; the Mizo hub is the aggregate of everything. Product feel: less margin, functional nav, left-side navigation. Each version below is a live click-through — no page reloads.

The Rail

Slim left icon rail: Mizo mark on top (hub), the six chains stacked below (commit = store takeover), Launch / Swap / Bridge at the foot. A right-docked swap/bridge widget is always visible. Big-cover launchpad grid in the center.

The Street

The hub is an isometric night street of storefronts, one shop per chain. Click a shop to “enter the store” (loading transition) into a full chain takeover; step next-door to switch chains. An aggregate leaderboard runs above the street.

The Deck

Dense terminal. Left drawer with a pinned committed-chain selector at the top: pick a chain and the whole deck reskins, pick “all chains” to aggregate. A persistent swap/bridge dock is one click from anywhere. Launchpad is the permanent hero.

The Switcher

Two left columns, Slack-style. A far-left chain switcher (Mizo home + six chain glyphs) fully commits to a chain; the second, contextual drawer swaps its nav to that store. Strong hub-vs-committed identity shift. Big-art launchpad in the center.
